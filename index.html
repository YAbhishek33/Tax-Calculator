?

?

💡 HRA Tips

Rent receipts are mandatory if rent exceeds ₹1 lakh/year. Keep monthly receipts with landlord's PAN.
Paying rent to parents? You can claim HRA exemption by paying rent to your parents! (They'll declare it as rental income though)
No HRA in salary? You can still claim deduction under Section 80GG if you're a salaried employee without HRA component.
Home loan + Rent? If you have a home loan on a property you don't live in, you can claim BOTH HRA and home loan interest deduction!

TDS — What You Need to Know

Check TDS in Form 26AS — Login to income tax portal → View Form 26AS. All TDS deducted is shown here.
TDS on Salary — Your employer deducts TDS monthly based on your estimated income. Submit investment proofs to reduce TDS.
Excess TDS? — If more TDS was deducted than your actual tax, you get a REFUND when you file ITR.
Form 15G / 15H — If your total income is below taxable limit, submit Form 15G (below 60) or 15H (senior citizen) to your bank to avoid TDS on FD interest.

Capital Gains — Key Rules

Equity LTCG ₹1.25L exempt: Long-term gains on equity/mutual funds — first ₹1.25 lakh is tax-free each year. Above that: 12.5% tax.
Tax Harvesting: If your equity LTCG is nearing ₹1.25L, sell and rebuy to "reset" the cost basis. Book profits tax-free each year!
Set-off losses: Capital losses can be set off against gains. STCL can offset STCG and LTCG. LTCL can only offset LTCG.
Carry forward: Unabsorbed capital losses can be carried forward for 8 years.

Gratuity — Important Facts

Minimum 5 years of service required. If you leave before 5 years, no gratuity.
Maximum tax-exempt gratuity: ₹20,00,000 (₹20 lakhs) for private sector employees covered under Gratuity Act.
If employer is NOT covered under Gratuity Act, exemption = Half month's salary × years (different formula).
Government employees get FULL gratuity tax-free with no upper limit.

Home Loan Tax Benefits

Section 24(b): Deduct up to ₹2,00,000/year of home loan INTEREST on self-occupied property.
Section 80C: PRINCIPAL repayment is counted under 80C (within the ₹1.5L limit).
First-time buyer bonus: Section 80EEA — extra ₹1.5L deduction on interest if loan was taken between 2019-2022 and property value ≤ ₹45L.
Let-out property: If you rent out the property, you can deduct the ENTIRE interest amount (no ₹2L cap)!
Joint loan advantage: If loan is joint with spouse, both can claim ₹2L interest + 80C principal deduction independently!

⚡ Penalty Guide — What Happens If You Miss Deadlines?

😰

Late ITR Filing

Penalty: ₹5,000 if income > ₹5L. ₹1,000 if income ≤ ₹5L. Section 234F.
Interest: 1% per month on tax due under Section 234A
Loss of benefits: Cannot carry forward capital losses if filed late!
⏰

Advance Tax Default

Who must pay: If your tax liability exceeds ₹10,000/year (after TDS)
Interest 234B: 1% per month if paid after March 31
Interest 234C: 1% per month for short/delayed payment of each instalment
📋

Late TDS / GST

TDS late deposit: 1.5% per month interest (not a penalty, it's interest)
TDS late return: ₹200/day late fee under Section 234E
GST late filing: ₹50/day (₹20 for nil returns) + 18% interest on tax
